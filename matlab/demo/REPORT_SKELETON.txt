   importrobot: URDF/Xacro/SDF -> rigidBodyTree (Robotics System Toolbox).
   robotScenario/robotPlatform/addMesh/show3D/move: Robotics System
   Toolbox, Introduced R2022a. move(platform,"base",motion16) with
   motion16 = [x y z vx vy vz ax ay az qw qx qy qz wx wy wz], quaternion
   w-first. addMesh has NO rotation name-value: yaw~=0 boxes need the
   "Custom" mesh path (current world is all yaw=0, verified).
   VideoWriter 'Uncompressed AVI' = uncompressed RGB24 (lossless);
   'Archival' = lossless MJ2; MPEG-4 NOT available on Linux. Default demo
   video is lossless; MPEG-4 is opt-in (make_ppt_figures, for slides).
