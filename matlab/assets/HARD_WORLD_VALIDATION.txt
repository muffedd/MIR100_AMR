WHAT WAS TESTED
- Repo stack as committed, launched from the documented procedure,
  no manual code edits. World: hard_world.sdf. Headless.
- Config under test: the repo Nav2 params with robot_radius 0.53
  replaced by a footprint polygon (8-vertex convex hull of the real
  collision outline including the 45-degree-rotated laser pods;
  enclosing radius 0.5988 m) in BOTH costmaps. inflation_radius
  unchanged at the repo's actual value 0.70 (tuning, not geometry).
- Guidance: WORLD-ORACLE waypoints (route file authored from known
  world geometry). This is NOT autonomous exploration.
- Pass/fail criteria were FIXED BEFORE the runs
  (scripts/recorded_run/TEST_CRITERIA.md): C1 all 15 legs status 4
  in order, C2 per-leg ground-truth (GT) arrival <= 0.35 m,
  C3 final GT <= 0.50 m of the true goal, C4 swept-footprint
  clearance > 0 vs all 65 wall boxes, C5 zero action-level aborts
  and <= 3 BT costmap-clear recoveries. No retrofitted passes.

  at least 14.5 cm of geometric margin to wall geometry. This is

  world exposes no contact sensors, so it is not a contact log.
- C5 FAIL: 6 BT costmap-clear recoveries (budget <= 3) and 1
  action-level abort.

  follow_path aborts while GT margin stayed >= 14.5 cm - the
  controller refused in its costmap belief; the >= 14.5 cm GT
  geometric margin is the only contact-related evidence (no contact
  sensors exist in this world).
  The exact costmap cause is UNDETERMINED: cost values and local

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
- Model characterization to date (docs/MODEL_VALIDATION.txt): mass
  accounting and straight-line odometry match ground truth closely;
  turn tests found the body UNDER-rotates vs wheels (147 deg on a
  first turn, 164 deg steady-state, vs 175 deg at the wheels -
  consistent with caster scrub, cause not proven). Single operating
  points only; the model is partially characterized, not blanket
  validated.
- On both trials the first legs were physically reached (GT
  confirmed), and no trajectory came closer than 9.2 cm to any wall
  geometry. Contact sensors do not exist in this world, so absence
  of contact is supported only by this geometric margin evidence.
- Hard-world guided navigation is NOT validated: neither trial
  completed the route, recovery budgets were exceeded, and the
  controller's refusal cause is undetermined without costmap
  records. Map-belief success is not physical arrival: SLAM belief
  drifted 0.34-0.39 m from ground truth once the robot moved.

REMAINING LIMITS
- No contact-sensor data exists in this world (recorded in-band);
  clearance evidence is geometric only.
- "Why the controller refused" needs costmap cost captures that
  were not recorded; do not claim inflation/geometry as the proven
  cause.
- Waypoints are world-oracle guidance; unexplored-map autonomy is
  not claimed. Swept clearance is sampled at 55.6 Hz with <= 0.42
  deg yaw steps (rotation-arc residual ~0.02 mm), not a continuous
  mathematical proof.
- Testing was stopped after two trials, one with each route
  (2026-10-26); both failures are preserved as the final state of
  this work.
