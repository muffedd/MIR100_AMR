================================================================
MiR100 SIMULATION MODEL - BOUNDED VALIDATION REPORT
Date: 2026-10-26. Model: mir100_ref. Tests ran at repo head 872d847;
this report and harness were added later (model files unchanged).
Scope: simulation self-consistency only (command -> odometry ->
Gazebo ground truth). No physical MiR100 data has been collected,
so this is not hardware or digital-twin validation.
================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO REPRODUCE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Tested environment: robostack (micromamba) ROS 2 Jazzy + Gazebo
Harmonic on Linux, Python with gz transport bindings (gz.transport13).
The /opt/ros/jazzy paths below are the native-Ubuntu equivalent; the
native apt path itself has not been run end-to-end by the author.

Terminal 1 (build once, then):
  source /opt/ros/jazzy/setup.bash && source install/setup.bash
  ros2 launch mir_description gazebo.launch.py gui:=false
Terminal 2 (bridges):
  source /opt/ros/jazzy/setup.bash && source install/setup.bash
  ros2 run ros_gz_bridge parameter_bridge \
    /clock@rosgraph_msgs/msg/Clock[gz.msgs.Clock \
    /cmd_vel@geometry_msgs/msg/Twist]gz.msgs.Twist \
    /odom@nav_msgs/msg/Odometry[gz.msgs.Odometry
Terminal 3 (harness: scripts/validate_model.py in this repo):
  python3 scripts/validate_model.py --mode straight --trials 3 --out /tmp/straight.csv
  python3 scripts/validate_model.py --mode turn --trials 3 --out /tmp/turn.csv

Method: the harness waits for /odom and ground truth to stream, then
waits a 2 sim-second baseline, publishes a constant cmd_vel profile
for a fixed number of SIM seconds (/clock), and lets the robot settle
3 sim-seconds. It logs cmd, /odom, and Gazebo ground truth (model
pose from /world/empty/pose/info via gz transport) to CSV and
compares odometry vs ground truth between pre-drive and post-settle
samples. A fresh-process check after the baseline fix measured
odom 1.966 / GT 1.971 m vs the recorded 1.980 / 1.985 m runs below;
expect ~1% run-to-run spread from startup timing, not exact
reproduction of recorded values.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. STATIC CONFIGURATION AUDIT (actual runtime SDF)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Source: xacro -> URDF -> "gz sdf -p" (what the simulator loads).
- Footprint collision box: 0.9 x 0.58 m. OEM spec sheet: 890 x 580 mm.
  NOT exact: the model is 10 mm longer. Spec source (full URL):
  https://howtorobot.com/sites/default/files/2021-02/MiR100%E2%84%A2%20_%20Mobile%20Industrial%20Robots_0.pdf
- Drive wheel radius 0.0625 m = OEM nominal 125 mm diameter (nominal
  match only; no tolerance comparison).
- Wheel separation 0.4452 m; no public OEM figure found; unverified.
- Collision outline INCLUDING sensor pods: front laser at
  (0.4288, 0.2358) and back laser at (-0.3548, -0.2352), each a
  0.16 x 0.16 m collision box (pod joints rotate them 45 degrees;
  rotated extent 0.591 m, unrotated 0.599 m - a consistent outline
  must cover both). Worst-case corner radius from the rotation
  origin: 0.599 m (front laser pod). The chassis box alone needs
  0.567 m although its half-diagonal is only 0.536 m, because the
  box center is offset +0.038 m ahead of the rotation origin
  (front corners at x=0.4876: sqrt(0.4876^2+0.29^2)=0.567). A geometry-consistent Nav2 robot_radius must be at
  least ~0.60 m; the default 0.53 leaves the laser pods uncovered.
- Masses as loaded: base_footprint lump 62.0 kg (base_link 58.0 +
  the two laser links at a declared 2.0 kg each); 2 drive wheels at
  1.0 kg; 4 caster assemblies at ~1.31 kg. Total 69.239 kg vs OEM
  70 kg: 1.1% under, coincidental (declared approximations, not
  tuned to the spec sheet). Laser 2.0 kg values are pre-existing
  declared approximations without a cited datasheet.
- Decorative mirref visual links are massless (lumped at zero mass;
  valid URDF, zero dynamics impact by construction).
- Inertias: uniform-box formula (base), cylinder formula (wheels),
  explicit tensor (caster bases). Approximations.
- Drive: gz-sim DiffDrive; max linear accel 1.0 m/s^2; max angular
  accel 1.5 rad/s^2; odometry publish rate 50 Hz (configured).
- Friction: the model specifies no surface friction. The SDF default
  is mu = mu2 = 1.0 (SDFormat specification, link > friction element:
  http://sdformat.org/spec?ver=1.11&elem=link). Whether Gazebo
  Harmonic's physics engine applied exactly these defaults was NOT
  independently verified at runtime.
- Scanners: 2x gpu_lidar, 541 samples, -2.2689..+2.3562 rad (~265
  degrees), 0.05-29.0 m, 12.5 Hz configured target (achieved rate
  not measured). Earlier live checks on this model: zero self-hits;
  obstacle response within 0.03 m of geometric prediction.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. RUNTIME RESULTS (measured)
----------------------------------------------------------------
STRAIGHT, 0.2 m/s for 10.0 sim-s, 3 trials (all identical):
  odometry 1.980 m | ground truth 1.985 m | odom-GT gap 0.005 m (0.25%)
  lateral drift 0.000 m | heading change 0.00 deg
  Command-vs-GT -0.75%, consistent with the 1.0 m/s^2 accel ramp.
TURN, 0.5 rad/s for 6.283 sim-s (commanded integral 180.0 deg):
  wheel-joint integral 175.0 deg; /odom 175.3 deg (wheel-vs-odom
  agreement 0.3 deg at this operating point).
  Ground-truth body rotation, 4 observed trials:
    first turn after straight travel: 147.2 deg, 147.3 deg (2 trials)
    subsequent turns (casters already tangent): 164.0, 164.0 (2 trials)
  Wheel/body disagreement: 28 deg on a first turn, 11 deg steady-state.
  Instrumented run: caster swivels approached the correct tangent
  angles (within ~7 deg) but started straight-travel-aligned and
  scrubbed while swiveling in; drive wheels tracked commanded
  velocity closely in cruise (1.781 vs 1.781 rad/s expected).
Interpretation (bounded): wheels and odometry agree at the tested
operating points. The wheel/body disagreement is CONSISTENT WITH
wheel-ground and caster scrub slip at the default friction; that
causality is not proven, and whether it matches real MiR100 physics
is unknown (no hardware data collected).

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. KNOWN LIMITATIONS (all open)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Rotation: the body under-rotates vs wheel odometry by ~6%
  steady-state and ~16% on the first turn after straight travel.
- Single operating points tested (0.2 m/s; 0.5 rad/s); no speed sweep.
- Hard-map guided route: NOT validated as a continuous run. In two
  DRIVER sessions against one long simulation (driver restart only,
  no sim restart): session one passed 4 legs by map belief, then its
  driver looped 559 repeat goals on one waypoint (driver bug: no
  increment-on-success while the robot sat 0.63 m from the waypoint,
  outside its 0.6 m skip threshold; why Nav2 instantly reported each
  repeat goal as succeeded is UNEXPLAINED - the goal tolerance was
  0.25 m and a rigid transform preserves distances, so a transform
  explanation is unproven). Session two passed 10 further legs by map
  belief. "Passed" means Nav2 reported success in its own map frame;
  motion was not continuously GT-validated. Final ground-truth
  distance to the route goal: 0.589 m. Waypoints were derived from
  ground-truth world geometry; unexplored-map autonomy is not claimed.
- Conservative hard-test params (robot_radius 0.60 / inflation 0.90)
  have never completed a route; the passing legs used the default
  0.53 / 0.70, which per section 2 does not enclose the sensor pods.
  (An earlier revision of this file said 0.55 - that was a reporting
  error; the repo's actual default inflation_radius is 0.70 in both
  costmaps. Geometry justifies enclosing the 0.599 m outline only;
  inflation is separate tuning, never geometry-dictated.)
- RECORDED RUNS 2026-10-26: two bounded pre-registered trials with a
  footprint polygon (0.5988 m hull) replacing robot_radius BOTH
  FAILED to complete the hard-world route (legs 4 and 5 aborted).
  Full record: docs/HARD_WORLD_VALIDATION.txt. Hard-world navigation
  is NOT validated.
- No physical data collected.
